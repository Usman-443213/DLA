Common Questions

We Love Questions.
Here Are the Common Ones.

In what states will my dealer license be valid?
You'll be able to access dealer-only auctions across the USA, Canada, Mexico, and Europe. You'll be able to retail or wholesale vehicles to anyone in the USA and export vehicles across the globe — giving you one of the most flexible and powerful dealership setups in the industry.
How long does the entire business and license setup process take?
Typically, we are able to complete your new business and dealer license setup within 30 days, start to finish. We have seen completed licenses come back in as few as 21 days. There are a number of variables involved — certain steps such as the pre-license seminar and background check require client completion, but we register you for both. Speed of licensure depends on how quickly those activities are completed.
Will a criminal conviction prevent me from getting licensed?
A criminal conviction does not automatically prevent dealer licensure. State licensing authorities consider the nature of the conviction and whether it relates to business honesty or trustworthiness. Violations and most convictions older than 7 years — especially misdemeanors — will generally not result in denial. However, state regulatory agencies maintain final review and approval authority and assess each application individually. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.
Is it legal to use dealer plates for personal driving?
According to the Department of Revenue, Motor Vehicle Division, dealer plates may be used for personal use by owners, officers, or employees of the business on vehicles owned by the business. Employees are defined as persons working for the business at least 36 hours per week. Regulations may vary by state — we'll walk you through the specifics that apply to your license.
What are the next steps to get started?
Getting started with Dealer Licensing Authority is straightforward. Simply reach out to us directly by phone — our team will connect you with a specialist who will guide you through the process from day one. Once you're on board, our licensing consultants begin immediately on setting up your business and managing your dealer license application from start to finish.
